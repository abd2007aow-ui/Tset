```
FORMAT     9:16 · 54 s · one continuous camera, 0 hard cuts; three fixed bands (title / diagram / subtitle)
STRUCTURE  hook question (two hypotheses) -> "how do we understand it?" -> build the case (injured, unconscious, can't speak or move)
           -> two observers (witness waiting for testimony / doctor checking the patient) -> each asks a different question x2
           -> the answer card ("still alive, question unanswered") -> "the difference between two states" (response stopped vs life ended)
           -> the text doesn't name a specific injury -> CTA stamp
HOOK       0-0.2 s fade in from dark; frame is already full: fixed big title question (2 colours), lying figure, card "انتهت حياته"
           and a "response" signal meter; at 2 s a yellow sticker "مات ثم عاد؟" pops in on the figure
VOICE      one narrator · Saudi/Gulf conversational dialect ("وش صار", "اللي") · calm, explanatory · speaks from 0.2 s · ~118 words in 52 s = 2.3 words/s overall, ~2.7 while speaking
           (counted from the on-screen subtitles; whisper model download was blocked)
CAPTIONS   full phrase (4-7 words), not word by word · bottom band, centre · dark serif (Naskh) regular · changes every 1.5-3 s with a short fade
           · two fixed credit lines under it (grey + red)
WORLD      flat infographic on old paper texture · palette #e0d8c7 #d9d1be #d2c9b6 #c9bdaa (paper), ink #2b2b2b,
           accents red #b5332a, teal #1f6f78, blue tags #3d6fb6, yellow sticker #f2c94c · even, flat light, low saturation (13/100)
CHARACTERS simple flat pictograms: a lying figure (skin + teal blanket), standing figures (doctor in blue, witness in purple)
           · small (about 1/6 of the width) · they don't move, they appear
CAMERA     fixed. No zoom, no pan. 97 % of the time nearly still; all change comes from things popping in
GRAPHICS   label cards with a red/teal number badge, the "response" bar meter, small coloured tags over people,
           a dotted line between the witness and the patient, two-option comparison cards with a check mark, scene title at the top of the panel
PACE       a new element every ~1.5-2 s; panel clears and rebuilds at scene changes (8 s, 10 s, 19 s, 30 s, 42 s, 52 s)
           · busiest moment: the fade-in at 0 s
SOUND      voice only (no steady music; beat strength 0.04) · -15 LUFS, range 3 LU · 17 % silence between sentences
END        last 2 s: panel goes empty, a red rubber-stamp "اشترك" lands in the bottom band (with a slight rotation)
DON'T COPY the channel name, the credits, the title question and the script; make your own topic and questions
```
